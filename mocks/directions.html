Brand exercise · 2026-05-05

Three directions, none of them Fraunces.

First pass landed on the warm-Fraunces-italic look that's become the default AI-startup brand in 2026 (Cursor, Vercel, Anthropic, half of YC). Resetting with three directions that are deliberately different from that default and from each other. Same content in each (PoolGo wordmark, dashboard widget showing free chlorine 2.4 ppm) so the comparison is apples to apples.

Sounds like

"In range. Nothing to do today." Five words max where four will do. ALL CAPS labels are fine here (ALL CAPS labels are blasphemy in the other two directions). Period at the end of every sentence is part of the brand.

Risk

Could read masculine/aggressive if not handled with care. Yellow accent + wit in the copy keeps it from going Bro Energy. Photography needs to feature actual people, not just objects, to balance.

How to react to this

You don't need to commit to one. The useful read is which direction makes you say "yes that's PoolGo" the loudest, and which one you can't see yourself running for five years. Three things to test against:

  • Is this PoolGo or someone else? Each direction should still feel like the company that runs Chart, Connect, Routines, and Discover. If a direction only fits one surface, kill it.
  • Will this still feel right when there are 50,000 users? Pool Club and Field Notebook are inherently boutique. Operator scales rougher but louder. Pick what you want at scale.
  • Does it work next to PoolrouteOps and Counter? The other ventures live in their own brand worlds. PoolGo doesn't have to match, but it shouldn't actively clash either.
