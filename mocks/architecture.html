PoolGo · architecture · 2026-05-07

How PoolGo aggregates a fragmented industry.

Before designing more screens, we lock the architecture. PoolGo aggregates four categories of stuff that don't normally talk to each other (equipment, automation cloud bridges, independent service businesses, and care records), plus an AI concierge that sits on top of all four. This page steals patterns from DoorDash, Google Home, and Alexa, then maps them to PoolGo's actual data shape and the screens we've already built.

References

What we steal from the three best aggregator apps.

All three keep a clean white surface and let the brands of the things they aggregate carry the color. None of them try to "out-design" the merchants/devices they're aggregating — they just organize them. Each contributes a different pattern.

DD
DoorDash
~200 illustrations · merchant logos prominent
Each merchant carries its own brand presence.
Logos, photos, color, and category tags from every restaurant or store appear on the home screen as themselves. DoorDash provides a consistent frame (illustration system, type scale, layout) but never the content — that comes from the merchant.
Apply to PoolGo: Pentair, Hayward, Polaris, Leslie's, Sun Country, Marina each show as themselves. PoolGo provides the frame, not the brand.
G
Google Home
Devices grouped by Spaces · Favorites tab first
Spaces (Cameras, Lighting, Wi-Fi, Climate) group devices by function, not maker.
A user looking for "the lights" sees lighting from Hue, LIFX, Lutron, Nanoleaf together — not by brand. A Favorites tab pins what you actually use. Each device is a tile that keeps its maker's identity but lives inside a functional Space.
Apply to PoolGo: Equipment groups by function (Water, Circulation, Heat, Light, Cleaning) not by manufacturer. Today/Favorites is the entry point.
A
Amazon Alexa
Shortcuts bar · Routines as core · single chat
Routines are first-class. Chat is everywhere as a single interface.
The shortcut bar at the top of every page surfaces popular Routines. A single agent chat is reachable from any screen. Filtering happens by device type (lights/locks/thermostats), and you can flip between List and Map views.
Apply to PoolGo: Routines as a top-level tab. Concierge chat reachable from anywhere. Filter equipment by Space + by maker.
The core

PoolGo aggregates 4 categories of things, plus 1 layer that connects them.

Every screen in PoolGo shows mixed instances of these four categories, plus AI-generated content. The activity feed, the dashboard, the My Pool view — they're all ways of slicing across these four. Locking the categories first means every future surface composes itself.

01
Equipment

The physical hardware sitting on your equipment pad and around your pool. Each item has a make, model, serial, and an OEM brand.

  • Pumps · Pentair, Hayward, Jandy
  • Filters · Hayward, Pentair, Jandy
  • Heaters · Pentair, Raypak, Hayward
  • Salt cells · IntelliChlor, AquaRite
  • Lights · IntelliBrite, ColorLogic
  • Cleaners · Polaris, Dolphin, Hayward
  • Sensors · ORP, temp, leak
02
Automation

The cloud bridges that give you remote control + telemetry over equipment. Each integration is a connection PoolGo speaks to on your behalf.

  • Pentair Home / IntelliCenter
  • Hayward OmniLogic
  • Jandy iAquaLink
  • Aqualink RS
  • Manual entry · always available
  • Pool tester apps · Sutro, BlueRiiot
03
Services

The independent people and businesses who actually touch your pool. PoolGo doesn't employ any of them — it just keeps your relationships straight.

  • Service techs · weekly route
  • Stores · water testing + retail
  • Builders · construction + warranty
  • Specialists · heater, plaster, salt cell
  • Inspectors · pre-purchase, insurance
  • Permits · city, HOA
04
Care records

The history of everything that's been done to your pool. The aggregation across the other three categories — the data layer.

  • Water tests · from stores or DIY
  • Service visits · checklists + notes
  • Equipment telemetry · live readings
  • Photos · technician + owner
  • Messages · with folks
  • Routines · your rules
P
PoolGo Concierge (the layer that aggregates)
Reads across all four categories. Drafts messages to your folks using your equipment data. Suggests routines based on your care records. Summarizes what's happening. Always your-side, never paid by OEMs or service businesses.
Reads · drafts · suggests · summarizes
Information architecture

Six top-level surfaces, each a different slice through the four categories.

Every tab is a different way of cutting across the same four-category data set. "Equipment" is the equipment slice. "Care" is the records slice. "Folks" is the services slice. "My Pool" is the cross-section showing today across all four. Routines and Discover are intentions.

My Pool
Default · today's slice
Today across all four categories. Pool photo + state pill + AI summary at top. Then: latest water test (Care), live equipment status (Equipment + Automation), folks-with-action (Services), and recent activity. This is the showroom-style page we've been iterating on.

Cat. 03 · services
Folks on your pool (existing relationships) lives at the top. Below: Discover — find new techs, stores, builders within X miles. Each business shows its own brand, photo, ratings, services, OEM brands they're certified on. Ad-free, vouched-for by neighbors, no commissions.

Visual system

Four tile shapes, one per category.

Like DoorDash has illustrations for food/home/retail and Google Home has device tiles, PoolGo has four tile shapes — one per category. Each tile carries the OEM color of whoever it represents. The tile shape tells you what KIND of thing you're looking at; the color tells you who it BELONGS to.

What this unlocks

Three next moves, in order.

Once the architecture is locked, every screen becomes a composition of the four tile types from the four categories. We don't have to invent new patterns — we just compose.

1. Equipment grid
Google Home Spaces idea, applied
Build the Equipment top-level page that groups your hardware by Water / Circulation / Filter / Heat / Lights / Cleaning. Each Space is a section. Each device tile uses the same Showroom card we've already designed. This proves the IA without inventing anything new.
2. Folks index
DoorDash merchant grid, applied
Top-level "Folks on your pool" surface that shows all four contact tiles in one grid (service tech, store, builder, specialist), each in their employer color, with the same tile shape. Discover sits beneath as the "find new" surface. We have the components — just compose.
3. Inbox
Alexa-style single chat, applied
Unified message thread surface where conversations with Anthony (Sun Country), Carlos (Marina), Jessica (Leslie's) sit side-by-side. Concierge can draft messages here. Each thread inherits the brand color of the recipient. This is where the relationship work actually happens — and we haven't built it yet.
