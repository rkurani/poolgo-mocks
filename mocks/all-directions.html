What's distinctive

The type IS the design. Massive numbers and headlines treated like architectural signage. Black hero bands top of every page, real California-pool photography in the body, orange and cobalt sparingly as functional accents.

Risk

Direction 06 · From references

Cinematic Mood

The Superpower zone. Dark cinematic surfaces, golden-hour gradients, italic display serif (Instrument Serif) over photography, almost no chrome on the homepage. The data UI lives in a separate inner-app surface that inherits the warmth without the moodiness.

References: Superpower · Levels · Hims (recent rebrand) · The Fasting Method · Equinox

What's distinctive

Marketing leads with mood and photography, near-zero UI chrome. Italic Instrument Serif (the warmer Fraunces alternative) carries the headline. Inner-app screens inherit the dark + golden-hour palette but go quiet so data can breathe.

Risk

Two-mode brand: marketing site cinematic AND product UI readable. Most companies fail at one or the other. Photography requirement is even higher than Architectural.

Six on the table. Three good shortlists.

Pick what's most useful as a next step.

  • Mature & data-friendly: Architectural (5) + Field Notebook (2). Both lean on real type, both can use real photography. Coherent grown-up brand.
  • Warm & approachable: Friendly Native (4) + Operator (3). Both punchier, both more app-feeling. The 2026-startup brand.
  • Destination-grade: Cinematic Mood (6) standalone. Highest production cost, biggest ceiling.
  • My read: Architectural feels like the strongest single bet of the six. Field Notebook is the most distinctive in the pool category. Pool Club feels like the one to retire.
